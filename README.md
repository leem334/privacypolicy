GoFlappy GO — PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: March 4, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION
GoFlappy Go ("the App," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Information You Provide
- Display Name: If you choose to submit a score to the global leaderboard, you provide a self-chosen display name. This is not linked to your real identity.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- Game Data: High scores, coins earned, skins purchased, and gameplay statistics are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is not transmitted to our servers except for leaderboard submissions.
- Leaderboard Data: When you submit a score, your chosen display name, score, and selected skin are sent to our server and stored in our database.
- Purchase Data: In-app purchase transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information.

2.3 Advertising Data
The App uses third-party advertising services (Google AdMob) to display ads. These services may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and ad interaction data in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
- To operate the global leaderboard feature
- To process and verify in-app purchases
- To display relevant advertisements
- To improve the App experience

4. DATA SHARING
We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:
- Apple Inc.: For processing in-app purchases and subscriptions
- Google AdMob: For serving advertisements
- Our server provider: For hosting leaderboard data

5. DATA RETENTION
- Local game data remains on your device until you delete the App
- Leaderboard entries are retained indefinitely to maintain the global rankings
- You may request removal of your leaderboard entry by contacting us

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Flappy Wings is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The only data collected (display name for leaderboard) is a self-chosen alias, not linked to real identity. No account creation is required.

7. DATA SECURITY
We use industry-standard security measures to protect leaderboard data transmitted to and stored on our servers. Local game data is stored on-device and protected by your device's security features.

8. YOUR RIGHTS
You may:
- Choose not to submit leaderboard scores (the feature is optional)
- Request deletion of your leaderboard data by contacting us
- Opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings
- Delete all local game data by uninstalling the App

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
